Practical advice

Painting guidance for Quebec homes

Simple answers to help you plan your project, choose better products and avoid costly mistakes.

Best paint colours for Quebec homes

Warm whites, greiges, soft greys, sage greens and natural tones work well in many Quebec homes. The best colour depends on light, flooring, cabinets and whether the goal is resale or comfort.

When to repaint exterior surfaces

Exterior painting needs dry surfaces, stable temperature and no rain in the forecast. Late spring, summer and early fall are usually the best windows in Quebec.

Interior vs exterior paint

Interior paint focuses on appearance, washability and low odour. Exterior paint is designed for UV exposure, moisture, freezing, thawing and material movement.

How long does paint last?

Interior paint can last 5 to 10 years depending on use and cleaning. Exterior paint varies more based on exposure, preparation, product quality and maintenance.
