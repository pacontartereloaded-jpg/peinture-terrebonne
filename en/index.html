Painting in Terrebonne

Clean work, clear communication and proper preparation

Peinture Terrebonne helps homeowners, landlords and businesses refresh their spaces with professional interior and exterior painting.

We focus on the details that protect the final result: surface prep, clean masking, product selection, weather-aware exterior work and a final walkthrough before the job is considered complete.

Painting services

Interior, exterior and light commercial painting

Great painting starts before the roller touches the wall. The surface condition, preparation and product choice decide how premium the finish will look.

01

Interior painting

Homes, condos and rental units: living rooms, kitchens, bedrooms, ceilings, staircases, doors, trim and mouldings with a clean, washable finish.

  • Walls, ceilings and trim
  • Floor and furniture protection
  • Preparation for uniform coverage
02

Exterior painting

We paint the exterior details that change curb appeal: doors, frames, balconies, stairs, fences, garages and facade elements.

  • Scraping, sanding and cleaning
  • Products suited for Quebec weather
  • Durable curb appeal upgrade
03

Light commercial painting

For offices, shops, clinics, rental units and professional spaces, we deliver a clean, coordinated finish without disrupting your operations.

  • Offices, corridors and reception areas
  • Scheduling around your needs
  • Durable finishes for traffic areas
Specialized services

Detailed painting work for a premium finish

Beyond walls, we handle the surfaces that most affect how a property feels: cabinets, stairs, railings, fences, trim and full preparation.

04

Kitchen cabinet painting

Refresh a kitchen without replacing everything. We assess the material, degrease, sand, prime and recommend a durable finish for daily handling.

  • Doors, boxes and side panels
  • Degreasing and adhesion preparation
  • Smooth modern cabinet finish
05

Surface preparation

Preparation is what protects the finish. We repair minor imperfections, sand rough areas, caulk joints, mask carefully and prime when needed.

  • Minor repairs and sanding
  • Clean masking around trim
  • Primer matched to the surface
06

Exterior stair painting

Stairs, stringers, risers, railings and guards need products that handle foot traffic, freezing, sun and humidity.

  • Porch and entrance stairs
  • Railings, balusters and guards
  • Durable exterior finish
07

Fence painting and staining

Wood, metal or previously coated fences need the right cleaning, scraping and coating choice to improve the yard without looking heavy.

  • Fences, gates and yard sections
  • Paint or stain depending on material
  • Protection against sun and moisture
08

Doors, trim and woodwork

Small details create a premium impression. Doors, frames, baseboards and yellowed woodwork can make a home feel much cleaner.

  • Sharp lines around frames
  • Washable finish for touch zones
  • Modernization without heavy renovation
09

Pre-sale and rental refresh

For selling, renting or turning over a unit, we focus on the surfaces that quickly show maintenance: marked walls, worn corners and traffic areas.

  • Clean quick refresh
  • Priorities based on budget
  • Clear quote from photos
